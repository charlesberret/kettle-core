# Brief framework

Use when writing or rewriting a queue entry `brief` (founding, restock, hand-file).

## Two movements (required)

1. **First line** — plain, jargon-free, immediately useful cold: what this document is and what is wanted of the patron.
2. **1–3 sentences** — precision only: specifics, terms of art, what to check, an exact quote or fault location. Must not restate movement 1.

## Constraints

- 2–4 sentences total.
- Spell out abbreviations on first use.
- No procedural agent tails (“check file X first”) — fold into the ask.
- Prefer `FAMILIARITY.md` over rediscovering settled structure; spend reading on what is new or uncertain.
- Never make the patron decode shorthand to learn why the file is in front of them.
